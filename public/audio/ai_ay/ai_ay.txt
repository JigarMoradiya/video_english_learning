Meet ai and ay — two best friends who make the same sound!
They both say... ayyy! Like in rain... and play!
But if they sound the same... how do you know which one to use?
Here's the secret — little i is very shy. She never sits at the end of a word, so ai stays in the middle.
But y is brave — y loves being last! So ay goes at the end of a word.
Let's try some more words! Snail... train... ai is in the middle. Play... stay... ay is at the end!
Now it's your turn! Here's the word... ai or ay?
It's ai — because it's in the middle!
Remember: ai in the middle, ay at the end. Easy, right?
