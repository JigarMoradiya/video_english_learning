Oy! Did you hear that sound? Oiii! That's the sound oi and oy both make!
You hear it in coin... and in toy. Same sound... different spelling!
So... how do we know which one to use?
Here's a simple trick! oi likes to stay in the middle of a word. That's why we write c-o-i-n... coin!
But oy likes to be at the end of a word. That's why we write t-o-y... toy!
Let's try some more! Soil... point... foil… oi is in the middle. Boy... joy... coy… oy is at the end!
Now it's your turn! Which one goes here? enj__... oi or oy?
It's oy! Because it's at the end!
Now you know! oi goes in the middle, oy goes at the end!
