c ⚡ k ⚡ ck meet the /k/ crew — one sound, three spellings L5 · Spelling Rules⏱ 2:19Video
  1. Here's something cool! c, k, and ck all make the same /k/ sound!
  2. Three spellings… one sound! Let's see how!
  3. Listen: c-at… k-ite… du-ck. /k/, /k/, /k/! Same sound, three different spellings.
  4. So how do we choose? It depends on where the sound is in the word!
  5. We use c most of the time. At the start of a word, before a, o, or u, we use c — c-at, c-ot, c-up!
  6. But there's one special rule! Before e and i, c says /s/, like in city. So when we hear /k/ instead of /s/, before e or i, we write k — k-ey, k-it, k-ing!
  7. And at the end of a word, right after one short vowel, we use ck — two letters, but just one /k/ sound! du-ck, ro-ck, ki-ck!
  8. Let's try some words! c: cat, cup, cot. k: key, kit, king. ck: duck, rock, kick!
  9. Now it's your turn! How do we spell duck? c, k, or ck? (pause)
  10. It's ck — because there's a short vowel right before it! 🎉
  11. Remember: c before a, o, and u… k before e and i… and ck after a short vowel at the end. Easy trick, right?

oo 🌙 ⚡ 📖 one spelling, two sounds — moon or book? L13 · Vowel Teams⏱ 1:54Video
  1. Here's a tricky one — the letters oo make two different sounds!
  2. Most of the time, oo makes a long sound… ooooo! Like in moon, zoo, and food. 🌙
  3. But some words use a short oo sound… /u/! Like in book, good, and foot. 📖
  4. Here's the tricky part! oo doesn't always sound the same. So when you're reading, try the long sound first. If it doesn't sound right, try the short sound!
  5. Here's a helpful hint! oo before k is often short… book, look, cook. But that's not always true. Good and foot are short too! So keep practicing, and trust your ears.
  6. Here are some examples!
  7. Long oo 🌙: moon, food, zoo, spoon.
  8. Short oo 📖: book, good, foot, wood.
  9. Now it's your turn! good… long or short? (pause)
  10. It's short — good! 📖 No k… but it's still short!
  11. Remember! oo is long most of the time, like moon. But some words are short, like book.
  12. The more you read, the easier it gets!
