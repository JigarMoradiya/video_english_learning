Oy! Listen to that sound. Oiii!
Two spellings make it. Oi, and oy.
You hear it in coin. And in toy.
Same sound, different spelling.
So which one do we write?
It's the same secret as always. WHERE the sound sits.
Beginning, middle, or end.
In the MIDDLE of a word, we write oi.
C. Oi. N. Coin!
Letters before it, letters after it.
At the END of a word, we write oy.
T. Oy. Toy!
Nothing follows it. So it's oy.
We never write toi. And we never write coyn.
Oi in the middle. Oy at the end.
Let's see it.
Coin. Soil. Point. Oil. Join. Boil.
All oi, all in the middle.
Now the end.
Boy. Toy. Joy. Enjoy. Annoy. Coy.
All oy, all at the end.
Your turn! Here's the word. Enjoy.
Is it oi, or oy?
It's oy! Because the sound is right at the end.
So remember. Oi sits in the middle, oy jumps to the end.
