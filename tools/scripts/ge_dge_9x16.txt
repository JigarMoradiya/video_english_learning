It is the same sound, but the spelling is different.
So how do we know which one to write?
Just like ch and tch, we look at the letter before it.
Here is the rule.
If the sound comes right after a short vowel, we write dge.
Listen.
B - a - dge.
Badge!
That a is a short vowel, so this word takes dge.

They all have a short vowel first, and then dge.
In every other place, we simply write ge.
There are two of those places.
The first is after a long vowel.
Listen to cage.
The a says its own name, so it is long.
That means we only need ge.

So let's remember it together.
After a short vowel we write dge.
After a long vowel or a consonant, just ge.
