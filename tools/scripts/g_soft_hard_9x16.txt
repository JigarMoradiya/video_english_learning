Look at this letter.
G.
G has two different sounds, just like c.
Listen to the first one.
Goat.
The g says /g/.
Now listen to the second one.
Gem.
The g says /j/.
The same letter, making two different sounds.
So how do we know which sound to say?
It is the same secret we used for c.
We look at the letter that comes after the g.
Here is the rule.
Do you remember our three special letters?
E.
I.
Y.
When g sees e, i or y coming next, the g goes soft.
A soft g says /j/.

Gem!
The e comes right after the g, so this g is soft.
So what happens everywhere else?
Everywhere else, the g stays hard.
A hard g says /g/, deep in your throat.
Listen.
g...
u...
m...
Gum!
The u comes after the g, and u is not one of our three letters.
So this g stays hard.
But now I must tell you something important.
Some words have e or i after the g, and the g stays hard anyway.
Listen to these.
Get.
Give.
Girl.
Gift.
Begin.
Tiger.
Every one of them should be soft, but it is not!
So learn them by sight, and they will never trick you.
