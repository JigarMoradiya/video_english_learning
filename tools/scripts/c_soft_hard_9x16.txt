Look at this letter.
C.
C has two different sounds.
Listen to the first one.
Cat.
The c says /k/.
Now listen to the second one.
City.
The c says /s/.
It is the same letter, but it makes two different sounds!
So how do we know which sound to say?
In our last videos, we looked at the letter before.
This time, we look the other way.
We look at the letter that comes after the c.
Here is the rule!
Three letters are special.
E.
I.
Y.
When c sees e, i or y coming next, the c goes soft.
A soft c says /s/, like a quiet little snake.
Listen.
/s/...
ih...
ty...
City!
The i comes right after the c, so this c is soft.
So what happens everywhere else?
Everywhere else, the c stays hard.
A hard c says /k/, strong and short.
Listen.
/k/...
a...
t...
Cat!
The a comes after the c, and a is not one of our three letters.
So this c stays hard.
Here are more hard c words.
Cup.
Cold.
Clap.
There is one more thing you should know.
Look at these five words.
Special.
Precious.
Musician.
Ancient.
Ocean.
Every one of them has a soft c.
This soft c does not say /s/.
It says /sh/.
Look at how each one ends.
-cial
-cious
-cian
-cient
-cean
When the c is followed by two vowels like that, it says /sh/.
That is a rule of its own, and it has its own video.
Now it's your turn!
Here is the word...
Cycle.
Is this c hard...
or is it soft?
It is soft!
The y comes right after the c.
Let's remember it together.
Before e, i or y, the c is soft.
A soft c usually says /s/.
Everywhere else, the c is hard and says /k/.
