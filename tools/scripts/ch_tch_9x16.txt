Here's a fun sound...
Ch! Ch! Ch!
This sound has two spellings!
We can write ch, or we can write tch.
You hear it in chair, and you hear it in catch.
Same sound, different spelling.
So which one do we write?
We look at the vowel right before it.
Here is the rule.
Right after one short vowel, we write tch.
Listen.
C - a - tch.
Catch!
That a is a short vowel, so we write tch.
Here are more.
Match.
Watch.
Fetch.
Most of the time, we just write ch.
At the start of a word, nothing comes before it.
Chair.
Chin.
After another consonant, like the n in lunch.
Bench.
After a long vowel, which says its own name.
Beach.
Coach.
All three take ch.
But watch out!
A few little words break this rule.
Much, such, rich and which.
They have short vowels, but they still use ch.
These are special words, so try to remember them!
You will meet longer ones too.
Ostrich.
Attach.
Now, it is your turn!
Here is the word.
Coach.
Do we write it with ch, or with tch?
It is ch!
Because o and a make a long vowel.
So let's remember it together.
After a short vowel we write tch.
Everywhere else we write just ch.
And don't forget those special words!
