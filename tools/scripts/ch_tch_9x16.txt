Listen to this sound.
Ch!
Two spellings make it.
We can write ch, or we can write tch.
You hear it in chair, and you hear it in catch.
Same sound, different spelling.
So which one do we write?
We look at the letter just before it.
Here is the rule.
Right after a short vowel, we write tch.
Listen.
C - a - tch.
Catch!
That a is a short vowel, so we write tch.
Here are more.
Match.
Watch.
Fetch.
Everywhere else, we simply write ch.
At the start of a word, nothing comes before it.
Chair.
Chin.
After a consonant, like the n in lunch.
Bench.
After a long vowel, which says its own name.
Beach.
Coach.
All three take ch.
But watch out!
A few little words break this rule.
Much, such, rich and which.
They have short vowels, but they still use ch.
Learn those four by heart.
You will meet longer ones too, like ostrich and attach.

Here is the word.
Beach.
Is it ch, or tch?
It is ch!
Because e and a make a long vowel.
So let's remember it together.
After a short vowel we write tch.
Everywhere else we write just ch.
