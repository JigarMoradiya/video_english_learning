Ohhh! Meet oa and ow.
Two spellings, one long O sound.
You hear it in boat. And in snow.
Same sound, different spelling.
So which one do we write?
Ask the same question. WHERE is the sound?
At the beginning, in the middle, or at the end?
In the MIDDLE, we write oa.
B. Oa. T. Boat!
Letters on both sides of it.
At the END, we write ow.
Sn. Ow. Snow!
Nothing after it. So we use ow.
We never write bowt. And we never write snoa.
Oa in the middle. Ow at the end.
Let's see it.
Boat. Coat. Goat. Road. Toast. Soap.
All oa, all in the middle.
Now the end.
Snow. Grow. Blow. Slow. Show. Yellow.
All ow, all at the end.
Your turn! Here's the word. Road.
Is it oa, or ow?
It's oa! The sound is in the middle.
So remember. Oa sails in the middle, ow rolls to the end.
