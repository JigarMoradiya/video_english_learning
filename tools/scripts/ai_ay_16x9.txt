Meet ai and ay. Two spellings, one sound.
They both say the same thing. Ayyy!
Listen. Rain. Play.
Same sound, different spelling.
So how do you know which one to write?
Here's the secret. It's all about WHERE the sound sits in the word.
Every word has a beginning, a middle, and an end.
When the sound is in the MIDDLE, we write ai.
Look. R. Ai. N. Rain!
There are letters before it, and letters after it. It's tucked right in the middle.
But when the sound is at the very END, we write ay.
D. Ay. Day!
Nothing comes after it. That's why we use ay.
So we never write rayn. And we never write dai.
Ai in the middle. Ay at the end.
Let's see it.
Rain. Snail. Train. Paint. Tail. Chain.
Every one has ai in the middle.
Now the end ones.
Day. Play. Say. Stay. Tray. Hay.
Every one finishes with ay.
Now it's your turn! Here's the word. Paint.
Is it ai, or ay?
It's ai! Because the sound is in the middle.
So remember. Ai hides in the middle, ay finishes the word.
