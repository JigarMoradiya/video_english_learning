WHERE the sound sits in the word.
Ou likes the beginning and the middle. Never the end.

So how do you know which sound to say?
Here's the honest answer. The letters don't tell you.

C-ow. Cow! There it is.
That's the trick. Try one sound, then try the other.
Keep the one that makes a real word.
