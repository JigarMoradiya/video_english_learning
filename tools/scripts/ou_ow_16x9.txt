Ouch! That sound.
Ow! Two spellings make it. Ou, and ow.
Say it with me. Ow!
You hear it in cloud. And in cow.
Same sound, different spelling.
So which one do we write? It's the same secret as always.
WHERE the sound sits in the word.
Ou likes the beginning and the middle. Never the end.
Look. Out. Cl-ou-d. H-ou-se.
Ou stays tucked inside the word.
Ow takes the END of the word.
C-ow. N-ow. H-ow.
Nothing comes after it.
And here's a bonus. Ow also works right before a final n or l.
Br-ow-n. T-ow-n. Ow-l.
Now listen carefully. This is the tricky part.
Ow has TWO different sounds!
In snow and grow, ow says the long O. Ohhh.
But in cow and brown, ow says ow!
Same two letters. Two different jobs.
So when you see ow, try both sounds. Snow? Or cow?
Pick the one that makes a real word.
Let's see them. Out. Cloud. House. Mouth. Round. Shout.
Every one has ou inside.
Now the ow words. Cow. Now. How. Wow. Brown. Owl.
Ow at the end, or guarding that final letter.
Your turn! Here's the word. Brown.
Is it ou, or ow?
It's ow! Because ow can sit right before a final n.
So remember. Ou hides inside. Ow finishes the word.
And ow always has two sounds to try.
