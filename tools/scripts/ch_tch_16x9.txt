Listen to this sound. Ch!
Two spellings can make it. We can write ch, or we can write tch.
Say it with me. Ch!
You hear it in chair, and you hear it in catch.
It is the same sound, but the spelling is different.
So how do we know which one to write?
This time we look at the letter just before the sound.
Here is the rule. If the sound comes right after a short vowel, we write tch.
Listen. C - a - tch. Catch!
That a is a short vowel, so this word takes tch.
Here are more. Match. Watch. Fetch.
They all have a short vowel first, and then tch.
In every other place, we simply write ch.
There are three of those places.
The first is at the start of a word.
Chair. Chin. Chip.
Nothing comes before the sound, so we write ch.
The second is after a consonant.
A consonant is any letter that is not a vowel.
Listen to lunch, and bench.
There is an n before the sound, so we write ch.
The third is after a long vowel.
A long vowel says its own name.
Listen to beach, coach and peach.
Those vowels are long, so we write ch again.
But watch out, because four words break this rule.
They are much, such, rich and which.
All four have short vowels, so they should take tch.
But they don't. They just use ch!
So why are they different?
Here is the funny part. These four words are older than the rule!
People were writing them a very long time ago.
The rule came later, but these four were already here.
So they never had to change.
There is no rule for these four. You just have to know them.
But here is a trick that makes it easy.
They go together in two pairs that rhyme.
Much and such. Rich and which.
Now let's look at more tch words.
Catch. Match. Watch. Fetch. Itch. Ditch.
Every one of them has a short vowel, and then tch.
And now let's look at the ch words.
Chair. Chin. Chip. Lunch. Beach. Coach.
At the start, after a consonant, or after a long vowel, we write just ch.
Now it is your turn! Here is the word. Beach.
Do we write it with ch, or with tch?
It is ch! Here, e and a make a long vowel, not a short one.
So let's remember it together.
After a short vowel we write tch.
Everywhere else we write just ch.
And don't forget our four old friends. Much and such. Rich and which!
